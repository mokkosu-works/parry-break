- A brand-new look: the whole game, plus the app icon and launch screen, is now drawn in 16-color retro pixel art
- New CONTINUE button on the title screen jumps straight into your next stage
- Stage select is now a map, with stages linked by a path. Turning chapters plays a sound
- How to Play has been redesigned: flip through one topic per page, each with an animated diagram
- Music now plays on the title and menu screens
- During OVERDRIVE, the background changes color and lights run around the edge of the screen
- Settings are now grouped into Controls, Sound, Display and Other tabs
- With the "Screen halves" controls, the side control hints are gone and the play area is bigger
- The results screen is easier to read, and the pause button is smaller and sits at the right end of the score bar
- The red dotted line that ends the run when blocks reach it is now called the DEAD LINE
- Fixed an issue where the music stopped after moving on to the next stage
- Minor bug fixes and performance improvements
